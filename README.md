# BFH Backoffice (Version 2)

**BFH Backoffice (Version 2)** is the enterprise Golden Record Case Management, Director Operations, Legal Authorization, NYC EDRS Rapid Filing, Livery Fleet Dispatch, and Aftercare CRM system for Benta's Funeral Home.

---

## 🏛️ Core Features

- **Director Active Cases Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into all active cases, phase trackers, urgent task lists, and immediate actions.
- **5-Phase Golden Record Pipeline:** Seamless progression through Intake/Removal, Arrangements & Contract, Legal Authorizations, Permits & Dispatch, and Finances/Aftercare.
- **Arrangement Contract & Form AP-47 Studio:** NY State Article 34 compliance, 12 standardized spaces, automated itemized fee calculation, and cash advances.
- **Legal Authorization Matrix & Canvas e-Signature:** High-security canvas-based signpad for NOK affidavits, embalming consents, and cremation authorizations.
- **NYC EDRS Rapid Fill Modal:** Direct payload generator and checklist for New York City Electronic Death Registration System submissions.
- **Woodlawn & Logistics Dispatch Engine:** Scheduled cremation and cemetery logistics integration with electronic manifests and permit bundles.
- **Livery & Fleet Management:** Fleet holds, scheduling, driver routing, and vendor dispatch.
- **Two-Way Service Partner SMS Dispatch:** Direct Twilio/Carrier SMS integration for florists, clergy, musicians, and transport partners.
- **Financial Verification Center:** ACH bank transfer reconciliations, life insurance claim assignments, and payment split tracking.
- **Director Scheduling & 1099 Roster:** Managerial oversight for licensed directors, trade staff, and 1099 disbursement tracking.
- **Facility & Room Calendar:** Multi-chapel booking, viewing parlor reservation, and clash detection.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 + TypeScript + Vite 6
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + PostCSS + CSS Variables
- **Icons & Visuals:** Lucide React, GSAP Animations
- **Deployment:** Vercel
